About

I am an ML systems engineer, TensorFlow core contributor, and final-year Computer Science student at the College of Engineering Munnar (APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University), building an independent AI lab dedicated to Sovereign Machine Intelligence.

I believe every individual deserves uncompromising privacy without diluting themselves into centralized systems, and every organization must own sovereign AI: models, data, and agent runtimes they govern locally, without leaking proprietary state to cloud monopolies.

Centralized AI stacks are fundamentally brittle and insecure. My lab addresses this across two co-designed engineering layers:

  • Agents and Harnesses That Do Not Break: Deterministic execution harnesses and runtimes with cryptographic event hashing, immutable action ledgers, and semantic checkpoints that guarantee zero side-effect duplication and verifiable recovery across multi-hour executions (creator of CONTINUUM and SNAGLINE).
  • Task-Specific Sovereign Models: Lean, domain-specialized architectures engineered specifically for the job, running privately offline or on-premises with zero external data egress (creator of QwerySmith and maya-healthcare-llm).

Since February 2025, I have conducted undergraduate research under Prof. Rajeev Rajan at GEC Idukki, working on computer vision, hybrid wavelet-attention architectures, and representation learning for rare disease diagnosis and video activity understanding (see Publications).

I have contributed 24 pull requests to TensorFlow across Grappler, XLA compiler shape lowering, and numerical gradients (see Code and technical essay), and I chair the IEEE Computer Society chapter at my college.

Currently focused on sovereign intelligence architectures and fault-tolerant agent runtimes, and open to research collaborations and engineering partnerships.
