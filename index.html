About

I am a final-year Computer Science and Engineering student at the College of Engineering Munnar (APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University), focused on AI/ML and open-source systems.

Since February 2025, I have conducted undergraduate research under Prof. Rajeev Rajan at GEC Idukki, working on computer vision, medical image analysis, and representation learning for rare disease diagnosis and video activity understanding (see Publications).

I build and maintain open-source tools for AI agent reliability: CONTINUUM, an execution and recovery layer for long-running agents, and SNAGLINE, a real-time failure detector. I have also contributed 24 pull requests to TensorFlow (21 merged, 3 approved; see Code).

I chair the IEEE Computer Society chapter at my college.
